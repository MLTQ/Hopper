# shared_folders.rs

Exercise the authenticated QR invitation endpoint, one-use redemption and two independent paired
desktops with a third player. Check folder boundaries and nested names, symlink exclusion,
actual byte ranges, duplicate registration, discovery after rescan, removal and continued access
through an independent replica. Camera decoding and physical iPhone playback require device checks.

A restart test reopens the same desktop identity, then moves its archive out of reach and back.
The designation survives while unavailable, and the source returns after a rescan.

The import-removal regression uses two same-named reference roots and a share with identical media. It checks local authentication, distinct source IDs, remaining catalogue coverage and original bytes.

The native picker regression copies a nested archive and verifies its original logical path after moving the source.
