# home.js

Home selection and podcast intake for the desktop. Desktop hosts remain selectable offline;
imports enqueue durable core deliveries. Apple show/episode links and RSS are accepted, or one
Castamatic OPML file (2 MB maximum). Never claim listening history or downloaded files migrate.
Render escaped delivery labels/messages, including failed and pending work. Changing Home only
routes future additions; existing owners and pending destinations remain unchanged.
